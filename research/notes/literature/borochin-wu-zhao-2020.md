# Borochin, Wu and Zhao (2020)

**The Effect of Option-implied Skewness on Delta- and Vega-Hedged Option Returns.** SSRN working paper (January 2020 version).

## Question
- How is option-implied skewness priced in the cross-section of hedged option returns?

## Data
- OptionMetrics options with CRSP stock returns, 1996 to April 2016.

## Method
- Daily-rebalanced delta-hedged and delta-vega-hedged portfolios.

## Evaluation design
- Not stated.

## Findings
- The call-return relation is read as investors' skewness preference, stronger with larger market beta and lower information quality.
