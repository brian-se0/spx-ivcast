# Wang, Hyndman, Li and Kang (2022)

**Forecast combinations: an over 50-year review.** arXiv:2205.04216v2.

## Question
- What is the state of the literature on combining forecasts since Bates and Granger (1969)?

## Data
- Not applicable; a survey.

## Method
- Covers point and probabilistic combination, time-varying weights, and open-source software.

## Evaluation design
- Not applicable.

## Findings
- Combinations are beneficial on average relative to constituent models; the forecast combination puzzle is among the issues surveyed.

## Limits and caveats
- Not specific to implied volatility.

## Use in this study
Relates to the thesis only as general background on combining forecasts from several competing models.
