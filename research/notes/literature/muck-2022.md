# Muck (2022)

**Arbitrage-free smile construction on FX option markets using Garman-Kohlhagen deltas and implied volatilities.** Rev. Deriv. Res. 25: 293-314.

## Question
- How can an arbitrage-free FX smile be built using FX market conventions?

## Data
- Market data for two days: a normal scenario and a stress scenario in April 2020.

## Method
- Approach of Carr (2016) adapted to delta quotation; ATM volatility matched by construction.

## Evaluation design
- Comparison with the Vanna Volga Approach, which does not guarantee arbitrage-free prices.

## Findings
- Differences appear only in the wings, and matter most under stress.

## Limits and caveats
- Two-day empirical illustration, FX only.

## Use in this study
Relevant only as an arbitrage-free smile construction method in a different asset class.
