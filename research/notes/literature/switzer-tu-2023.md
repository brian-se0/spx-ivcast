# Switzer and Tu (2023)

**The Impact of Position Limits on the Relationship between Option Trading and Stock Performance: Evidence from the SPY pilot program.** SSRN preprint (September 2023).

## Question
- How do position limits affect the link between option trading activity and the returns and volatility of the underlying?

## Data
- SPY and SPY options, Oct 2010 to Dec 2020, around the pilot suspension of limits (CBOE Rule 4.11 amendment).

## Method
- Option volume shocks related to SPY return volatility shocks, compared inside and outside the pilot period.

## Evaluation design
- Not stated.

## Findings
- Volume shocks raise SPY volatility shocks less when limits are suspended; market tracking performance of ETFs improves.
