# Skintzi and Fameliti (2025)

**Combining realized volatility estimators based on economic performance.** J. Asset Manag. 26: 819-846.

## Question
- Does weighting combined volatility forecasts by the economic decision they serve improve on statistical-loss weighting?

## Data
- Various realized volatility estimators of the S&P500.

## Method
- Time-varying combination weights from three economic performance measures.

## Evaluation design
- Out-of-sample comparison with robustness checks and extensions.

## Findings
- Superior economic performance relative to single estimators and statistical combinations.

## Limits and caveats
- Not stated in the parts read.

## Use in this study
Not directly related: forecast combination of S&P500 realized volatility, not the implied-volatility surface.
