# Zhan, Han, Cao and Tong (2022)

**Option Return Predictability.** The Review of Financial Studies 35(3): 1394-1442.

## Question
- Which stock characteristics predict delta-hedged equity option returns?

## Data
- OptionMetrics U.S. individual stock options, 244-month sample.

## Method
- Cross-sectional characteristic sorts.

## Evaluation design
- Portfolios net of transaction costs.

## Findings
- Profits can be explained by two option factors.

## Limits and caveats
- Single-stock option returns, not surfaces.

Not directly related.
