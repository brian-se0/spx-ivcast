# Käfer, Mörke and Wiest (2026)

**Option Factor Momentum.** Journal of Financial and Quantitative Analysis 61(1): 32-60.

## Question
- Do option factors exhibit momentum?

## Data
- OptionMetrics IvyDB, 1996-2021 data with sample period January 1999 to December 2021.

## Method
- Factor momentum portfolios formed on 28 characteristics-based option factors.

## Evaluation design
- Portfolio performance with robustness checks.

## Findings
- Option factor momentum subsumes option momentum but not vice versa.

## Limits and caveats
- Equity option returns, not implied-volatility surfaces.
