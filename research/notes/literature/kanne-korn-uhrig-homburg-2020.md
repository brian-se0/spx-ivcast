# Kanne, Korn and Uhrig-Homburg (2020)

**Stock Illiquidity and Option Returns.** SSRN working paper (December 2020 version).

## Question
- How does the underlying stock's illiquidity affect option returns?

## Data
- OptionMetrics Ivy DB, January 1996 to August 2015.

## Method
- Option returns conditioned on end-user demand; tested against common risk factors, stock volatility and option spreads.

## Evaluation design
- Not stated.

## Findings
- Premium magnitudes are in line with reasonable transaction-cost assumptions; results are robust across illiquidity measures and data periods.
