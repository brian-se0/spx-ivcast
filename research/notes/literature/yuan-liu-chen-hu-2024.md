# Yuan et al. (2024)

**Option Trading Volume and the Cross-Section of Option Returns.** SSRN preprint.

## Question
- Does option trading volume predict the cross-section of option returns?

## Data
- Daily OptionMetrics Ivy DB prices, January 1996 to December 2022 (324 months), with filters as in Cao and Han (2013).

## Method
- Portfolio strategies built on option trading volume.

## Evaluation design
- Not stated.

## Findings
- Strategies earn significant returns; market capitalization and idiosyncratic volatility are examined as explanations.

## Limits and caveats
- Not peer reviewed.
