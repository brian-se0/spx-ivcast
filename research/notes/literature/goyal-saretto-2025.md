# Goyal and Saretto (2025)

**Can Equity Option Returns Be Explained by a Factor Model? IPCA Says Yes.** The Review of Financial Studies 38(6): 1783-1821.

## Question
- Can a factor model explain returns of delta-hedged equity option strategies?

## Data
- Equity option returns over 1996-2022 with contract and stock characteristics.

## Method
- IPCA reduces the dimension of many characteristics.

## Evaluation design
- Alpha of 46 long-short strategies before costs.

## Findings
- The model can serve as a benchmark for other option portfolios.

## Limits and caveats
- Explains returns; no forecast of implied volatility.
