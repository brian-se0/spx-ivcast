# Alexiou et al. (2025)

**Pricing event risk: evidence from concave implied volatility curves.** Rev. Finance 29(4): 963-1007.

## Question
- Does concavity of the IV curve before scheduled earnings reveal bimodal risk-neutral distributions and priced event risk?

## Data
- Short-term equity options, 2013-2020; 38 percent of IV curves become concave before earnings days versus 4.8 percent otherwise.

## Method
- Concavity as an ex-ante signal, related to announcement-day returns, post-announcement realised volatility and straddle, strangle and calendar-straddle returns.

## Evaluation design
- Not stated.

## Findings
- Concave-curve firms show larger absolute announcement returns and lower straddle returns.

## Limits and caveats
- Single-stock earnings events, not index surfaces.
