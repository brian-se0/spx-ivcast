# Muravyev and Pearson (2020)

**Options Trading Costs Are Lower than You Think.** The Review of Financial Studies 33(11): 4973-5014.

## Question
- Why is options volume high when conventional trading-cost estimates are large?

## Data
- Nanex trade and quote data for options on S&P 500 stocks, 2004-2015.

## Method
- Shows high-frequency option price changes are predictable and measures execution timing.

## Evaluation design
- Not applicable.

## Findings
- Conclusions about after-cost profitability of option strategies change.

## Limits and caveats
- Single-stock options.

## Use in this study
Relates to option transaction-cost assumptions, not to forecasting the SPX surface.
