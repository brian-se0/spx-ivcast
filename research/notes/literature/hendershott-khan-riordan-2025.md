# Hendershott, Khan and Riordan (2025)

**Option Auctions.** The Review of Financial Studies (2025), advance article.

## Question
- How do wholesaler auctions for retail options orders work and affect price improvement?

## Data
- Auction and trade data, August to December 2020.

## Method
- Empirical study of auction behaviour and price improvement.

## Evaluation design
- Not applicable.

## Findings
- Most auctions do not have multiple bidders offering meaningful price improvement.
