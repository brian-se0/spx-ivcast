# Jacobs, Ke and Pan (2025)

**Tail Risk Around FOMC Announcements.** Journal of Financial and Quantitative Analysis (2025).

## Question
- Do option-implied tail risks explain the market risk premium around FOMC announcements?

## Data
- OptionMetrics daily option prices, 1996-2021.

## Method
- Option-implied moments used as predictors in return regressions.

## Evaluation design
- In-sample and out-of-sample.

## Findings
- Predictability lasts up to 1 week and is stronger for expansionary monetary policy shocks.

## Limits and caveats
- Uses implied moments as predictors of returns, not as forecast objects.

## Use in this study
Relates only as an example of option-implied information used in prediction.
